# The Money Map

The Money Map is a one-page method for seeing where money moves through a business, what the business is worth, what is holding it back, and the one move to make first. It comes from the book *Forget AI. Show Me The Money.* by Aamir Qutub.

This repository holds the method as a skill for AI assistants, so a business owner can be coached through a Money Map using their own numbers.

## The method in brief

Every business runs down the same lines:

**Orders won → Revenue realised → minus Cost to deliver → Margin → minus Overhead → Earnings → × Multiple → Worth**

Cash runs underneath as timing. It is never added to earnings or worth.

- **Worth = Earnings × Multiple.** Growth counts twice: once in earnings, then again through the multiple.
- **Three engines move the lines.** The Rainmaker brings the money in. The Producer makes and delivers the work. The Multiplier builds what the business is worth.
- **Cutting cost is the small, capped prize.** The bigger prize is growing what the business earns and what it is worth, on roughly the same cost base.
- **AI is pointed at the person, never at the customer.** It takes the admin, drafting, chasing and checking off the key person, so they can do more of the work that earns.

## What a session produces

A one-page Money Map with: the money lines for a normal year; worth shown three ways; the one constraint and the evidence for it; the bottlenecks, sized as ranges; the one move and where AI does the work; and a three-question start test with a first step and a review date.

## How to use it

- **No installation:** open [coach.forgetaibook.com](https://coach.forgetaibook.com) and follow the instructions there. This works with any AI assistant that can read a web page.
- **Claude Code:** copy the `skills/money-map` folder into your `~/.claude/skills/` folder, then ask Claude to build your Money Map.
- **Other assistants:** give the assistant `SKILL.md` and the `references/` folder, and ask it to coach you through your Money Map.

## What it is not

The Money Map is a thinking tool. Its figures are estimates from your own inputs. The worth figure is a scenario on an assumed multiple, not a valuation, and not financial, legal or tax advice. The worked examples from the book are illustrative. Check important decisions, and the multiple, with an adviser who knows your industry.
